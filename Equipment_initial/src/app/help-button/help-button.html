Scanner Quick Guide

Welcome to the Image Scanner Prototype. Use this tool to upload high-resolution images and convert them into editable text.

upload_file Upload

Upload documents using the Choose Files button or by activating the camera and taking a picture.

 auto_fix_high Extraction

Extract the text from the uploaded images by clicking the Scan Images button in the preview panel. This will automatically perform the extraction on all files.

 camera_enhance_icon Camera

The Camera can be activated by first clicking the Give Camera permission button, giving the app permission to access your device camera when prompted, selecting your device's camera in the dropdown, and then clicking the Start Camera button.

 inventory_2 Inventory

The Inventory panel shows a list of recently uploaded images and their extraction results. Click on an entry to expand it and see a more detailed view. Use the Download As button to save the entry as a file on your device. Use the Delete button to permanently delete the entry from your inventory.

 edit Edit

Edit the extracted text by clicking on or hovering over the desired image to edit and clicking on the Edit button that appears in the bottom right corner of the image. This will provide a suite of tools, which include:

  • crop Cropping: A cropping box will appear on top of the image in the editor that will allow you to move, shrink, and enlargen it to find the perfect size for your image. You can also use a mouse wheel or pinch to zoom controls to zoom as well.
  • zoom_out_map Zooming: A slider bar will appear at the top of the editor. Sliding it to the left will zoom out, and sliding it to the right will zoom in. Additionally, you can use the zoom_in and zoom_out buttons to zoom in and out.
  • rotate_right Rotating: A slider bar and controls will appear at the top of the editor for rotation. Using the rotate_left or rotate_right buttons will rotate the image 90 degrees in that direction. Using the slider will allow you to quickly rotate the image. Additionally, the add and remove buttons will allow for precise adjustment in 1 degree increments.
